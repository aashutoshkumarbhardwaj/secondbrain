## Post 1: Main Feed Post

### Visual Concept
Minimal aesthetic: dark background, warm amber text, single diya/lamp emoji as divider

### Caption
🌙 *Raat Ki Baatein*

Chandni ne pucha: "Itni raat gaye kyun jaage ho?"
Maine kaha: "Sapne poore karne hain, neend toh baad mein bhi aayegi."

Chand muskuraya: "Theek kaha. Jo raat bhar mehnat kare, uski subah zaroor hoti hai."

—

Kabhi kabhi raat sirf sojne ke liye nahi hoti.
Kabhi kabhi wo hoti hai — khud se milne ke liye.
Khud se waada nibhane ke liye.
